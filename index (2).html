Terms and privacy

The short version: it’s yours to use for as long as it works in your browser, I look after it, and your data never leaves your device.

In plain words
  • One purchase, no subscription, no expiry date.
  • I actively look after every tool for at least 12 months after your purchase, and usually much longer.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. What you enter stays in your browser.
  • Please keep a backup file. I can’t restore your data because I never have it.

Terms of use

What you get

A personal licence to use the tool you bought, for you and your household, on as many of your own devices as you like. Please don’t share your access code publicly or resell the tool.

How long you can use it

The tool is yours to keep using for as long as it works in your browser. There’s nothing to renew and no date when it stops.

Updates and help

For at least 12 months after your purchase I actively look after each tool: I fix bugs, keep it working with new phones and browser versions, and answer your questions through Etsy messages. In practice I plan to keep improving the tools for much longer, and updates are always free. What I can’t promise is that a tool will work with every future device for ever, because browsers keep changing.

Your data and backups

Everything you enter is stored only in the browser on your device. That keeps it private, and it also means nobody else keeps a copy. Make a backup file now and then, especially before you change phones or clear your browser data. The tools remind you.

Provided as it is

The tools are provided as they are, without promises beyond those your local law gives you. Weather figures, statistics and seating suggestions are made for personal use. Please don’t rely on them where exact accuracy matters, for example for safety or insurance.

Limits

As far as the law allows, my responsibility is limited to the price you paid for the tool. Nothing on this page limits the rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country.

Refunds

Digital downloads can’t be returned. If something doesn’t work for you, send me a message on Etsy and we’ll sort it out together.

Privacy

What I collect

Nothing. There’s no account, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. I never see what you write or who is on your guest list.

Where your data lives

In your browser’s storage on your device. Backup files and spreadsheets you save go only where you put them.

Hosting

The tools are delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address to send you the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts and the spreadsheet maker are served from this same site, not from third parties.

Rain or Shine Almanac: weather and place search

To fill in the weather and find your town, the diary sends your town’s coordinates to MET Norway (today’s weather), NASA POWER (earlier days) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (place search). Your diary entries are never sent.

Shared links

If a tool lets you share a view-only link, the shared data is stored inside the link itself. Anyone who has the link can see it, so send it only to people you trust.
